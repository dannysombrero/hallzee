# Hallzee help

Hallzee is a classroom hall-pass system with a keypad terminal by the door and
a Windows or Mac app for teachers. Choose the section that matches what you
need to do.

## Teachers: start here

- **New to Hallzee?** Follow the [teacher guide](Getting-Started-Users) to
  download the app, connect a terminal, add students, and use Hallzee during
  class.
- **Need the app?** Open the [desktop downloads](https://github.com/dannysombrero/hallzee/releases?q=client-v&expanded=true).
- **Something is not working?** Use the
  [teacher troubleshooting table](Getting-Started-Users#when-something-isnt-working).
- **Need an update?** The teacher guide explains both
  [desktop and terminal updates](Getting-Started-Users#update-the-desktop-app).

Teachers do not need Git, .NET, Arduino tools, or a GitHub account. Hallzee
keeps classroom records on the teacher's computer and continues recording
trips when Bluetooth or the internet is unavailable.
